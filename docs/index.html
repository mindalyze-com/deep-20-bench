Executive summary

A focused test of adaptive reasoning.

Deep20Bench tests whether a model can turn broad knowledge into a useful sequence of questions, retain the answers, and commit to an exact guess.

01

What it measures

Question quality, use of prior answers, and success before the turn limit.

02

Why it matters

Useful systems must often clarify uncertainty before they can act well.

03

What it does not claim

This is a narrow task benchmark, not a general ranking of model intelligence.

How the benchmark works

Simple rules. Controlled answers.

1

Ask

The Guesser asks up to 50 counted yes-or-no questions.

2

Check

An Oracle researches each question. A Reviewer checks every YES or NO. A blind Judge resolves disagreement.

3

Score

Lower is better. A failed trial contributes 51 questions. Subjects carry equal weight.

Core control

The model under test sees only the game.

It receives the category, its prior actions, and final YES, NO, or UNKNOWN tokens. It never sees private adjudicator prompts, evidence, searches, or hidden subject data.
