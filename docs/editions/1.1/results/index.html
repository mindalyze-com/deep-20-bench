Score distribution

Question score.

Lower is better. The blue marker is the average question score. The line is its 95% confidence interval (CI); darker lines are wider. The side plot shows each CI width in three equal bands.

Question score

The average number of questions the model needed, with every subject weighted equally. Lower is better. A failed round receives the fixed failure score.

Models rank by exact average score, then by narrower 95% confidence interval (CI). Equal scores and CI widths share a rank.

CI width

The line shows the 95% confidence interval (CI) of the average score. A narrower CI means more consistent results across repeated rounds on these subjects.

The side plot shows each CI width. Its three shaded bands split the shown range into equal parts. They are a visual aid, not quality grades.

The CI describes uncertainty in the average, not the range of single rounds.

Solved and format

Solved is the share of scored rounds in which the model found the subject. Failed rounds are already part of the question score. Valid format is the share of model replies that used the required format.

The 95% CI comes from repeated rounds on this edition's subjects. It does not cover other subjects, model versions, or providers. The three CI width bands split the shown range into equal parts; they are not quality grades. All models, A to Z →
