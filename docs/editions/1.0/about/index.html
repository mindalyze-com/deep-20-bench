Origin · news · prior work

A shared idea, built into a benchmark.

Patrick Heusser and Markus Tuor came up with Deep20Bench while playing Twenty Questions with the kids. Patrick then designed and built the benchmark.

Updates

What’s new.

New models on the leaderboard, releases, and articles. A score is the average number of questions a model needs, and missed rounds count as a penalty. Lower is better.

14 October 2026

Gemini 4 Argon (high) matches the lowest average score.

It scored 13.7 in edition 1.1 (lower is better), the same as GPT-6 Astra (high): both used 410 questions over 30 rounds and guessed every subject. Astra ranks first because its 95% confidence interval is narrower. Argon was the best of 20 models on Door handle and second on Albert Schweitzer and Bike pump, but 19th on Moon, which took it 11, 12, and 14 questions.
